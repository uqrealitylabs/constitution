# Event Policy

This policy takes effect when adopted by resolution of the management committee under the Constitution's "Events and Workshops" clause. The committee may amend it by resolution.

## Approval and planning

- The committee shall appoint one member as Event Lead for each event, including the proposer where appropriate, and record the appointment and any conditions of approval.
- Before approval, the Event Lead shall provide the event's purpose, audience, expected attendance, date and venue, proposed budget and funding, equipment needs, and principal safety risks and controls. The Event Lead shall obtain any required venue, University or Union approvals before the event proceeds.
- A detailed plan is required if expected attendance is at least 20, proposed expenditure exceeds $200, the event spans multiple days or is held off campus, an external organisation is involved, or the committee identifies elevated risk. The plan shall include an itemised budget, roles and roster, venue and equipment arrangements, risk controls and an incident contact.
- The Event Lead shall seek committee approval for a material change to the approved venue, scale, budget or risk before proceeding with that change. Event approval does not override the Constitution's rules for expenditure.

## Equipment

- The Event Lead shall record equipment issued for an event, its condition, the person receiving it and its return. A separate loan requires a written record of the item, condition, borrower and return date, and the borrower's agreement to care for it, return it, report any loss or damage, and reimburse reasonable unrecovered costs caused by their misuse or negligence.
- Loss or damage shall be reported to the committee promptly. Before seeking compensation, the committee shall consider the evidence of causation, the equipment's prior condition, fair wear and tear, any applicable loan terms, insurance recovery and the reasonable unrecovered cost of repair or replacement. The person concerned shall have an opportunity to respond. The committee may seek no more than the reasonable unrecovered cost from a person it finds responsible and shall record its reasons. Serving as Event Lead alone does not establish liability.

## Incidents

- The Event Lead shall first arrange immediate assistance and take reasonable steps to prevent further harm. For an emergency on a UQ campus, call [UQ Security](https://about.uq.edu.au/safety-and-security/emergencies) on **+61 7 3365 3333**; for an emergency off campus, call **000**. For a non-emergency security matter on campus, call UQ Security on **+61 7 3365 1234**. Follow the venue's emergency directions.
- The Event Lead shall promptly tell the committee of an injury, illness, near miss, safety hazard or material property loss. For an incident or hazard on a UQ site, assist the affected person to report it through [UQSafe](https://resources.policies.uq.edu.au/resources/health-safety-and-wellness/manage-health-and-safety-uq/report-incident-or-hazard); if they cannot, someone with access and first-hand knowledge shall report it. Follow any applicable UQ or venue reporting requirements for an off-campus event.
- For a report of sexual misconduct, bullying, harassment or discrimination, direct the affected person to [UQ Safer Communities](https://about.uq.edu.au/safety-and-security/sexual-misconduct-bullying-harassment-discrimination-support/report-incident) where applicable. Do not treat a committee incident record as a substitute for that process.
- Record the facts known, action taken and any external report made. Restrict personal details to those who need them for care or reporting. Do not assign fault in an incident report; any equipment compensation decision follows the process above.
