# Finance Policy

This policy takes effect when adopted by resolution of the management committee under the Constitution's "Funds and Accounts" clause. The committee may amend it by resolution.

## Account

- The [Union's February 2026 banking guide](https://uqu.com.au/wp-content/uploads/How-to-Complete-the-CBA-Banking-Authority-Form.pdf) identifies the Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Toowong branch. The Treasurer shall confirm the current direction before opening an account or changing authority. Account numbers and credentials shall be kept in secure club records, not this policy.
- Only the President, Secretary and Treasurer may be authorised as club representatives on the account. Union staff signatories required by Union regulations shall also be retained. The Treasurer shall arrange prompt removal of outgoing officers and addition of incoming officers using the current Union and bank forms. The account shall require two signatories for each transaction; no debit card or shared credentials are permitted.
- Money received shall be deposited into the club account promptly. Club funds shall not pass through a member's personal account.

## Spending and reimbursement

- Committee approval shall identify the purpose and spending limit. If urgent expenditure is authorised by two officers under the Constitution, the Treasurer shall put it before the next committee meeting for ratification.
- Before any payment, two disinterested officers shall record approval of the actual payee, amount and purpose. Bank transactions must also meet the bank's and Union's signatory requirements. Blank or incomplete payment instructions shall not be approved.
- An officer is not disinterested if they are the claimant or payee, or have a direct or indirect financial interest in the payment.
- A member claiming reimbursement shall provide the purpose, amount, evidence of payment and a physical or digital invoice or itemised receipt. The claimant shall not approve the claim or authorise its payment.
- If no invoice or receipt can be obtained, the claimant shall provide a statutory declaration stating the transaction date, payee, amount, purpose and reason the document is unavailable, with any other available evidence. Two disinterested officers may approve the exception and reimbursement in writing, recording their reasons and any Union requirements. The claimant shall not be one of them.
- If fewer than two eligible officers can approve a payment, it shall wait. A vacant office may be filled under the Constitution; another member shall not act in place of an officer merely because an officer is absent.

## Records

- The Treasurer shall record each receipt and payment in a ledger with its date, amount, purpose, payer or payee, supporting document and approvals. The Treasurer shall reconcile the ledger to bank statements monthly and report discrepancies to the committee.
- Invoices, receipts, declarations, approvals, statements and audit records shall be kept securely and made available for the annual return and audit.
